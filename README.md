# Global Electronics Retailer: Sales & Customer Analytics

## Overview of the Project
In this project, I analyzed sales transactions, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and store performance for a global electronics retailer.

Using Python, MySQL, Jupyter Notebook, and Tableau, the aim of the project is to transform raw retail data into actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Business Problem

The retailer needs a clearer understanding of its sales performance, its best-performing products and stores, and the purchasing behavior of its customers.

I investigated opportunities to improve sales performance, understand customer contribution, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Investigate customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales performance across stores.
- Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

## Dataset

The project uses the Global Electronics Retailer dataset from Maven Analytics which contains six CSV files:
- Customers
- Data Dictionary
- Exchange Rates
- Products
- Sales
- Stores

The data dictionary guided the interpretation of fields and relationships between tables.
